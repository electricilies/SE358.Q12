# Retrospective

## What went well

- Team members cooperated effectively and demonstrated responsibility for assigned tasks.
- All members adopted Atlassian Confluence as the unified documentation platform, improving accessibility and version control of project artifacts.
- Key planning artifacts (e.g., meeting minutes, communication plan) were produced and stored centrally for review.

## What could have been better

- Initial task assignment was delayed due to a personal issue; this impacted early momentum.
- There was no designated temporary lead when the team lead was occupied, which reduced the speed of decision-making.
- Progress monitoring could be more consistent: deadlines and milestone checks were not enforced uniformly, causing occasional late deliveries and unpublished artifacts.
- Some deliverables were completed but not published to Confluence in a timely manner, affecting traceability.

## What will we do differently

- Appoint a deputy lead who can assume coordination responsibilities when the lead is unavailable.
- Establish a weekly short sync (15–30 minutes) to review progress, surface blockers, and confirm upcoming deadlines.
- Require that all deliverables be uploaded to Confluence within 48 hours of completion and follow a standard template.
- Distribute tasks more evenly and record clear owners and due dates in Jira to improve accountability.
