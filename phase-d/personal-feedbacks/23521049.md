# Personal Feedback – Nguyễn Thái Gia Nguyễn

## Knowledge

During the Electricilies project, I gained a much deeper understanding of backend development, especially in designing and implementing RESTful APIs for product, cart, and payment modules. I learned about authentication and authorization using Keycloak, and explored advanced architectural patterns such as Domain Driven Design and Clean Architecture. These concepts were new to me, so I had to invest extra time in studying their principles and how to apply them effectively. I also improved my knowledge of database management, caching strategies, and integrating third-party payment gateways like VNPay.

## Skills

My technical skills grew significantly throughout the project. I became proficient in integrating external libraries, handling complex database transactions, and optimizing backend performance with caching. I learned how to document APIs using Swagger, which improved collaboration with the frontend team. My communication skills also improved as I regularly updated my progress and discussed blockers with the team. Time management and problem-solving became essential as I took responsibility for challenging tasks and adapted to changing requirements.

## Experience

This project presented many challenges, such as dealing with poorly documented libraries, understanding complex architectures, and working under tight deadlines. I felt the pressure of delivering features on time and sometimes had to refactor code to improve quality. Collaborating with other team members taught me the importance of clear communication and teamwork. I learned to ask for help when needed and to support others by sharing documentation and technical insights. Overall, the project helped me grow as both a developer and a team player.

## Use of AI

I used AI tools mainly for researching technical documentation, generating prompts for reports, and supporting API testing. AI helped me save time by providing quick answers and suggestions, especially when I was stuck on a technical issue or needed to draft documentation. However, I always reviewed and adapted the AI-generated content to ensure it was accurate and relevant to our project. I found that AI was a useful assistant, but human judgment and customization were still necessary for high-quality results.
